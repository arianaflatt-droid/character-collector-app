Binder Control Desk

Today

A new day gives every student a fresh set of keeps and can start everyone's XP back at 0. What they've already kept stays in their binder.

Add students

Keep only certain students

Added too many names? Click the names you want to keep (they turn green), then remove everyone else.

Paste XP for your class

Select the whole table on the XP page, copy it, and paste it here. For each student it reads the number before the slash (the 291 in "291 / 120"). Pasting the same day again just updates the numbers, and students who aren't in the paste are left alone.

Update XP from a spreadsheet

Tip: "Set" replaces their XP with the number in the file. "Add" stacks it on top of what they have.

Student links

Every student has their own private link (use "Copy link" on their row). To get them all at once, copy the list and paste it into a Google Sheet, or download it as a file.

Add a character

Find the picture, right-click it, choose "Copy image address", and paste it below. Links from wiki sites last longer than links from image search.

Add characters from a spreadsheet

Columns can be in any order: name, series, rarity (optional), and image link. The links need to be plain text in the cell.

Add many at once

One per line: Name | Series | Rarity | Image link. Tabs also work, so you can paste straight from a spreadsheet.
